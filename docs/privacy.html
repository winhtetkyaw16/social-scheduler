Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

SocialCastly is a tool for scheduling and publishing posts to the social accounts you choose to connect. SocialCastly is operated by Win Htet Kyaw. This policy explains what information SocialCastly handles, why, and how you can have it deleted.

What information we handle

  • App account email. The email address you provide when you create a SocialCastly account.
  • OAuth tokens. Access tokens, and refresh tokens when a platform issues them, for each social account you connect. Tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to call that platform on your behalf.
  • Connected account metadata. Profile name, handle or username, avatar, and account, page, board or channel IDs needed to show the account and publish to it.
  • Posts, schedules, and drafts. Captions, media, scheduled times, and publishing status for content you create in SocialCastly.

SocialCastly receives a platform’s tokens and account data only after you start a connection for that platform. If you do not connect an account, SocialCastly does not receive that platform’s data.

Why we use it

We use this information only to operate your SocialCastly account and to schedule and publish content to the platforms you connect: signing you in, showing the accounts you linked, saving drafts, publishing a post at the time you choose, and recording whether publishing succeeded.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use platform data for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Data from one platform is sent only to that platform, and only to publish what you asked SocialCastly to publish. Each platform processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

How long we keep it

OAuth tokens and connected-account metadata are kept while that account stays connected. Posts, schedules, and drafts are kept while they remain in your SocialCastly account. When you disconnect an account, its tokens are deleted. When you delete content or your account, the related data is deleted.

Data Deletion

  • Disconnect in the app. Open Manage Accounts in SocialCastly and disconnect any account. This deletes the stored OAuth tokens for that account immediately.
  • Request deletion by email. Email socialscheduler2610@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Data deletion request”. We will delete your SocialCastly account and all associated data, including tokens, connected-account metadata, and posts, within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Facebook / Instagram / Threads. If you remove SocialCastly from your Facebook settings, Meta notifies our data-deletion callback at https://api.socialcastly.com/api/v1/accounts/facebook/data-deletion, and we delete the associated data and provide a confirmation code you can use to check the status.

You can also revoke SocialCastly’s access at any time from each platform’s own security or connected-apps settings.

Security

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest, and data is transmitted over HTTPS.
