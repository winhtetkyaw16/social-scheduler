SocialStudio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

SocialStudio is a content studio for scheduling and publishing posts to social accounts you choose to connect. This policy describes the development version (MVP) of the application in the social-scheduler project, operated by Win Htet Kyaw. Practices may change while the product is in development. The date above is the date of this version.

What information we handle

Depending on how you use SocialStudio, the app handles:

  • App account email. The email address you provide when you create a SocialStudio account, if you provide one.
  • OAuth tokens. Access tokens, and refresh tokens when a platform issues them, for each social account you connect. These tokens let SocialStudio call that platform on your behalf.
  • Connected account metadata. Information the platform returns when you connect, such as account, page, board, or channel names and identifiers needed to show the account and publish to it.
  • Posts, schedules, and drafts. Captions, media references, scheduled times, and publishing status for content you create in SocialStudio.

SocialStudio receives a platform’s tokens and account data only after you start a connection for that platform. If you do not connect an account, the app does not receive that platform’s data through SocialStudio.

Why we use it

We use this information to operate your SocialStudio account and to schedule and publish content to the social platforms you connect. That includes signing you in, showing the accounts you linked, saving drafts, sending a post at the time you choose, and recording whether publishing succeeded.

If you connect LinkedIn, SocialStudio uses the OAuth tokens and the profile or organization details LinkedIn returns for the permissions you approve, and it sends LinkedIn the post content you choose to publish there. That information is used to connect the account and to schedule or publish your content.

We do not sell this information.

Third parties

When you connect an account, SocialStudio shares data with that platform through its OAuth flow and APIs. What is shared is what that platform’s API and the permissions you grant require in order to connect the account and to publish the content you submit. Each platform processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

The platforms SocialStudio can connect, only when you connect them, are:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram)
  • Google (YouTube)
  • LinkedIn
  • X
  • TikTok
  • Pinterest
  • Threads

Content and account identifiers are sent to a platform so that platform can publish what you asked SocialStudio to publish. They are not sent to a platform you have not connected.

How long we keep it

OAuth tokens and connected-account metadata are kept while that account stays connected. Posts, schedules, and drafts are kept while they remain in your SocialStudio account. When you disconnect an account, or delete content or your account, the related tokens and stored content are removed from the service, or will be removed when you ask us to delete them.

This is a development MVP. Storage, disconnect, and deletion behavior may change as the product changes. If a control is not available in the app yet, use the contact below and we will delete the SocialStudio data we can identify as yours.
